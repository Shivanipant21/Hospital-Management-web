About MAX Hospital

Two decades of trusted healthcare, compassion, and medical excellence in New Delhi.

Who We Are

MAX Hospital, New Delhi, has been a beacon of healthcare excellence since 2005. We are a multi-specialty hospital committed to delivering the highest standard of medical care to every patient who walks through our doors.

Our team of 12 specialist doctors, supported by trained nurses and paramedical staff, works tirelessly to ensure accurate diagnosis, effective treatment, and compassionate care.

With 100 beds, state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment, and a 24/7 emergency unit, we are equipped to handle everything from routine check-ups to complex surgeries.

  • ✅ NABH Accredited Hospital
  • ✅ 12 Specialist Departments
  • ✅ 100-Bed Capacity with ICU
  • ✅ 24/7 Emergency & Ambulance Services
  • ✅ Digital Patient Records System
  • ✅ Senior Citizen Discount Program
